How it works

The process

Three clear steps. Manual verification throughout. A focused five-day cleanup—not ongoing maintenance.

01
You share the list
Send a short description or a few sample records. We confirm scope before any work begins.
02
You receive a free preview
A small set of fully checked listings so you can see the format, notes, and level of care.
03
Full delivery in five days
Manual checks against public sources. Clear notes on anything that cannot be confirmed. Clean file ready for your team.
Five-day milestone map
Day 1
Intake, confirmation of scope, free preview delivered
Day 2 – 3
Source checks, name • address • phone consistency, status review
Day 4
Notes on unverifiable items, final structuring of the file
Day 5
Delivery of the cleaned list and acceptance of remaining balance
