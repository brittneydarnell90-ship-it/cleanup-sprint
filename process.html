COMMON QUESTIONS / 03

A few useful answers.

Clear expectations before you send your directory.

What does the $195 pilot sprint cover?

One manual cleanup of up to 30 resource listings, including checks of available contact and service information, consistent formatting, and notes on details that cannot be confirmed. The free three-record preview comes before you approve paid work.

Can you work with my existing directory format?

Yes, standard spreadsheets and CSV files are suitable starting points. I confirm the fields and final layout with you before the sprint. If you need a custom format, we will agree on it before work begins.

Will the cleaned directory work with our database?

The cleaned file can be prepared with agreed column names and a CSV or spreadsheet layout. Compatibility with a specific platform depends on its import rules; direct database integration is not included in the pilot.
