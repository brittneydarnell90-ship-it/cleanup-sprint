WHAT THE WORK INCLUDES

Reliable directory cleanup, without the confusion.

Cleanup Sprint focuses on the practical side of referral information: checking it, organizing it, and making it easier for your team to use.

01 / VERIFY

Data verification

I manually check the details in your directory, including contact information, service availability, and listing accuracy. The goal is simple: fewer dead ends and cleaner information.

  • Contact details reviewed
  • Broken or outdated information flagged
  • Listings cleaned for consistency
02 / ORGANIZE

Referral infrastructure

I help shape your listings into something staff can use more easily. That means better formatting, cleaner categories, and a structure that supports clear referrals.

  • Easier-to-scan listing structure
  • Cleaner service categories
  • More usable internal organization
03 / SUPPORT

Verified partner networks

When your information is clearer, your referral network becomes stronger. Reviewed listings help your team make referrals with more confidence and less guesswork.

  • Stronger partner visibility
  • More dependable referral handoffs
  • Greater confidence in shared information
